Submission rules

Websites that pass the automated checks can be published immediately. These rules protect visitors and keep the directory free from spam and prohibited content.

Rules every submission must follow

  1. Submit only a website you own or officially represent.
    Do not submit another person’s website without permission.
  2. Use the main homepage.
    The address must begin with HTTPS and must not point to an article, product, download, login page or other internal page.
  3. No affiliate, referral or tracking links.
    Links containing referral codes, campaign parameters, partner IDs or shortened redirects are rejected.
  4. Maximum two submissions each day.
    The limit applies separately to the submitted email address and the visitor’s internet connection.
  5. No duplicate domains.
    A domain may appear only once, including active, rejected or suspended records.
  6. No pornography or explicit adult services.
    Porn sites, explicit sexual content, sex-cam services, escort directories and similar adult websites are prohibited.
  7. No unsafe, deceptive or unlawful content.
    Do not submit phishing, malware, forced downloads, fraud, illegal goods, gambling services or websites that encourage harm.
  8. No misleading descriptions.
    Explain clearly what the website provides. Do not impersonate another organisation or make false guarantees.
  9. No low-effort spam.
    Doorway pages, copied websites and pages created mainly to manipulate search rankings are prohibited.
  10. The website must work.
    It must load successfully, use HTTPS and not redirect visitors to a different domain.
  11. Listings remain subject to removal.
    A listing may be suspended, edited or removed when reports or later checks show that it breaks these rules.

How automatic approval works

The system checks the submitted URL, HTTPS availability, redirects, duplicate domains, daily limits, common affiliate patterns, Turnstile verification and high-confidence prohibited-content signals. A website that passes is published immediately.

Automated screening cannot understand every website perfectly and cannot guarantee that a site will remain unchanged. Visitors can report problems, and the operator may suspend or remove listings after publication.

Fair placement and donations

Basic listings are free. Donations are optional and never guarantee publication, featured placement, a higher position or continued inclusion.
