Submission rules

These rules protect visitors and stop the directory becoming a collection of spam, redirects and paid-placement links.

Rules every submission must follow

  1. Submit only a website you own or officially represent.
    Do not submit another person’s website without permission.
  2. Use the main homepage.
    The address must begin with HTTPS and must not point to an article, product, download, login page or other internal page.
  3. No affiliate, referral or tracking links.
    Links containing referral codes, campaign parameters, partner IDs or shortened redirects are rejected.
  4. Maximum two submissions each day.
    The limit applies separately to the submitted email address and the visitor’s internet connection.
  5. No duplicate domains.
    A domain may appear only once, including pending, approved, rejected or suspended submissions.
  6. No unsafe or unlawful content.
    Do not submit phishing, malware, forced downloads, fraud, illegal goods, explicit adult content or websites that encourage harm.
  7. No misleading descriptions.
    Explain clearly what the website actually provides. Do not impersonate other businesses or make false guarantees.
  8. No low-effort spam.
    Automatically generated doorway pages, copied websites and pages created mainly to manipulate search rankings may be rejected.
  9. The website must work.
    It should load successfully, use HTTPS and not redirect visitors to a different domain.
  10. Moderation decisions are final.
    Listings may be edited, suspended or removed when they break these rules or create a risk for visitors.

What manual review means

A moderator checks the submitted homepage, description and category. Approval means the listing met the directory rules at the time of review. It is not a guarantee, certification or endorsement of the website.

Fair placement

Basic listings are free. Payment, donations or personal relationships do not guarantee approval or a higher position in the directory.
