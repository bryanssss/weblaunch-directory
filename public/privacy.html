Privacy

Privacy notice

The directory processes only the limited information needed to publish eligible websites, enforce daily limits and reduce abuse.

Information processed during a submission

The form receives the website name, homepage address, description, category and submitter email address. The email address is transformed into a one-way cryptographic hash before it is stored. The application does not retain the raw email address in D1.

The visitor’s IP address is also transformed using a private server-side salt. Only the resulting hash is stored. It is used to enforce the two-submission daily limit and reduce repeated abuse.

Automatic homepage inspection

When a website is submitted, the Worker requests a limited portion of the public homepage to check availability, redirects, page type and high-confidence prohibited-content signals. This temporary response is analysed during the request and is not saved as a copy of the webpage in the directory database.

Turnstile

The submission form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to distinguish normal visitors from automated spam. Cloudflare may process technical information required to provide that security service.

Published listing information

When the automatic checks pass, the website name, homepage URL, domain, description, category and publication date become public. Do not place personal information in the public description.

Contact and listing reports

The public contact form sends the visitor’s name, reply email address, subject, message and any listing reference privately to the directory operator through Cloudflare Email Service. The destination inbox address is not shown on the public website or included in the browser code.

The message body is not stored in the directory database. A one-way hash of the sender’s email address and IP address is used temporarily to enforce the daily contact-form limit. The operator may retain the received email in the destination mailbox for as long as reasonably needed to respond, investigate a report or protect the directory.

Retention

Active, rejected and suspended domain records may be retained to prevent repeated duplicate submissions and to operate the reporting and removal system. Daily limit counters are cleaned after a short period. The operator may delete records when they are no longer needed.

External websites

Listings link to independent external websites with their own privacy practices. WebLaunch Directory does not control how those websites collect or use information.

Your choices

Do not submit private information in a public listing description. Use the private contact form to request a correction or removal, and do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Owner note: This general notice is a practical project template, not legal advice. Add the operator identity, contact details and any wording required by the applicable jurisdiction before a wider public launch.
